  Takeaway: at 100-300 GHz free-space loss alone is ~70-110 dB at 1-20 m, about 25-35 dB worse than 6 GHz at the same distance (loss grows with frequency squared). Molecular absorption is negligible at room scale but grows with range, especially near the 183/325 GHz water-vapour lines. Practical 6G THz use therefore needs high-gain directional antennas and line of sight - short-range 'innovation zone' pilots, not wide-area coverage.

Takeaway: India's 500 MHz supports 1 x 320 MHz OR 3 x 160 MHz clean channels - enough for gigabit-class home/office Wi-Fi, but dense multi-AP buildings see more co-channel reuse and lower average SINR than fully delicensed markets such as the US (compare the Monte Carlo means above).
