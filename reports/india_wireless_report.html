  Takeaway: THz path loss grows so fast with distance and frequency that 6G THz use cases stay confined to very short range, line-of-sight 'innovation zone' pilots rather than wide-area coverage.

Takeaway: India's 500 MHz still supports one 320 MHz channel OR three clean 160 MHz channels — enough for gigabit-class home/office Wi-Fi, but dense multi-AP buildings will see more co-channel reuse and lower average SINR than in fully-delicensed markets like the US.
